Odia Phonetic Typing

Type it how it sounds. Works offline — this whole page is one file.

What to type

Case is ignored, so Ta and ta behave the same.

Picking a word. Any word with more than one plausible spelling is underlined with dots. Tap it and you get the whole-word options, numbered — tap one, or press its number key. The same roman can be several different Odia words (kala is କଳା, କାଳ, କଲା …), so you choose rather than fight the default. Your choice is remembered, and that word leads the list next time. The dashed button on the end keeps the English spelling, for names and acronyms.

About ଡ଼. This letter is everywhere in Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଶା, ଓଡ଼ିଆ, ବଡ଼) so you should not have to work for it. Common words are recognised automatically, and elsewhere a d in the middle of a word offers ଡ଼ as an alternative — tap the dotted word to pick it. Type R if you want it outright.

Conjuncts form on their own: type consonants back to back and the halant is inserted for you. namaskaara → ନମସ୍କାର, ksha → କ୍ଷ, bhaagya → ଭାଗ୍ୟ.

Double the vowel to lengthen it. This is the most common reason a word comes out wrong. a is ଅ but aa is ଆ, so ଭାଗ୍ୟ is bhaagya, not bhagya. Likewise ii/ee for ଈ and uu/oo for ଊ. Very common words are recognised either way.

ଯ and ୟ are different letters. Odia ଯ sounds like ja — the opposite of the Hindi intuition — while ୟ is the true ya. Typing y gives ଯ at the start of a word (yoga → ଯୋଗ) and ୟ anywhere else (raajya → ରାଜ୍ୟ). Tap the word to swap.

ny and gy are treated as two letters, because that is what they nearly always are (dhanyabaada → ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ). For the single letters ଞ and ଜ୍ଞ type ~n and jny.
